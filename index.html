How it works
  • Person alone: the minutes a person needs for one question, turned into hours, times their hourly wage.
  • AI + checking: what the AI charges per task, plus the person's time to check every answer, plus the time to fix the share of answers that are wrong. All of that people-time is paid at the local wage.
  • Savings: how much cheaper (or more expensive) AI + checking is compared with the person alone.
  • Break-even wage: the AI price stays the same everywhere, but the time AI saves is only worth as much as local wages. The break-even wage is where the saved time exactly pays for the AI price. Above it, AI wins; below it, the person is cheaper. If checking and fixing take as long as doing the task, AI can never break even.
  • Verdict: SCALE when AI is cheaper and its error rate is within your limit. FIX when only one of those is true. STOP when neither is.
